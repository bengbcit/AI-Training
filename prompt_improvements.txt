Explanation:
The original prompt "tell me about AI" is extremely broad and vague. It gives the AI no specific direction, which could lead to a generic, overwhelming, or irrelevant response. The improved prompt addresses these issues by:

1.  **Adding Specificity:** Instead of a vague "tell me about," it asks for a "comprehensive overview" and then explicitly lists the key components to cover (definition, concepts, applications, impact, ethics). This guides the AI to provide a well-rounded and targeted answer.
2.  **Clarity of Intent:** It clearly outlines what information is expected, removing ambiguity and ensuring the AI focuses on relevant aspects of AI.
3.  **Ensuring Structure and Depth:** By breaking down the request into numbered points, it implicitly encourages the AI to structure its response logically, making it easier for the user to digest the information. It also ensures a certain depth by requiring coverage of multiple facets of AI.
4.  **Defining Audience/Tone:** Specifying "accessible manner suitable for a non-technical audience" ensures the language used is easy to understand, avoiding overly technical jargon and making the explanation more useful to the intended user.
